Privacy · MiMiz Daycare for iPad and iPhone

The daycare's register, and no one else's.

MiMiz Daycare is a sign-in and sign-out app used at a daycare's front desk. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We operate no servers and receive no personal data from the app. The register is saved in the daycare's own private iCloud, and that is the whole story.

Effective: November 10, 2026

  • Nothing entered in MiMiz Daycare is sent to us. We have no server.
  • No analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising, no tracking identifiers.
  • The register is stored in the private iCloud database of the Apple Account signed in on the daycare's device.
  • The developer cannot see, read or download it.

What the app stores

The daycare enters each child's name, group, parents' names and, if it chooses, a photo. At drop-off and pickup the app records the time, the name of the adult signing, their drawn signature, and any note. Staff may record an absence and its reason.

Where it is stored

This information is saved in the private iCloud database of the Apple Account signed in on the daycare's device. It is available on other devices signed into that same account. Apple stores it under its iCloud terms.

The developer of MiMiz Daycare cannot see, read or download this information. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and sends nothing to the developer or any third party.

Reports and backups

Staff can create attendance reports and backup files and send them by email or save them. The daycare chooses the recipients and is responsible for those copies.

Access

The staff screens open only with the device's Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Face ID is used only for that unlock, and it is handled by the device; the app never receives face or fingerprint data.

Keeping and deleting information

The daycare controls the register and how long it is kept. Parents and guardians who want to see, correct or remove information about their child should ask the daycare. Deleting the app's iCloud data from the device's iCloud settings removes the register.

Children

The app is used by daycare staff and by parents and guardians, not by children. Information about children is entered by the daycare and stays in the daycare's iCloud account as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
