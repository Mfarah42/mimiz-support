Support · MiMiz Daycare for iPad and iPhone

The sign-in sheet, signed on screen.

MiMiz Daycare replaces the paper sheet at the front desk. At drop-off and pickup a parent finds their child, types their name and signs on the screen. Staff see who is here at a glance, and every entry is saved with its time and signature. No account, no tracking, and the register stays in the daycare's own iCloud.

FAQ

Frequently asked

The questions that come up most, answered plainly.

What do I need to use the app?

An iPad or iPhone signed into an Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and an internet connection. There is no separate account or password for the app itself.

How do I set it up the first time?

Open the app and choose First-time setup. Tap Staff, unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode, and add each child under Roster with their name and at least one parent's name. The lobby is then ready for parents.

How does a parent sign a child in or out?

On the lobby screen, tap the child, type your name, sign in the box and confirm. Do the same at pickup to check out. Each entry is saved with the time and the signature.

How do staff record an absence?

From the Staff screens, mark the child absent for the day and add the reason. The child then shows as absent on Today instead of having no entry.

Who can open the Staff screens?

Anyone who can unlock the device with Face ID, Touch ID or its passcode. Parents using the lobby cannot see the roster, records or backups.

Can we use more than one iPad?

Yes. Sign every device into the same Apple Account and the register appears on all of them. A device signed into a different Apple Account sees its own separate register.

How do I get attendance records out of the app?

Under Staff → Records, pick a day and export it as a PDF or a spreadsheet. There is also an end-of-day report ready to email.

How do I back up the register?

Under Staff → Backups, create a full backup file and save or email it somewhere safe. The same screen restores a backup. iCloud keeps the register in sync, but a backup file is your own copy, so make one regularly.

The app says it cannot save. What should I check?

Check that the device is online, that it is signed into iCloud in Settings, and that iCloud storage is not full. If it still fails, email us with the message shown on screen.

Does MiMiz Daycare collect any data?

No. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours. The register is stored in the daycare's private iCloud database, which the developer cannot see.
